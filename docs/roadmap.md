# Veekay Labs

## Product Vision

Build an AI-native platform that showcases my professional journey, demonstrates the products I build, shares my knowledge, and evolves into an interactive AI Product Management learning ecosystem.

### Core Pillars

- Me
- Build
- Think
- Learn
- Ask AI

### Product Evolution

Veekay Labs evolves through four distinct identities:

1. Professional Profile
2. Living Builder Platform
3. AI Product Management Knowledge Hub
4. AI Product Management Learning Ecosystem

Each release builds on the previous one without replacing it.

---

# Version 5.0 — Veekay Labs Platform

## Goal

Become a complete AI Product Management ecosystem where professionals can learn, build, experiment, and collaborate.

## Deliverables

- AI Product Sandbox
- Interactive Product Simulations
- Prompt Playground
- Evaluation Playground
- Community Marketplace
- Open Source PMOS
- Research Lab
- Public APIs

### Primary Audience

Everyone interested in Product Management and AI Product Management.

### Primary Outcome

Become a trusted destination where people can explore, build, learn, and collaborate using conversational AI as the primary interface.

## AI Platform Vision

The AI Assistant becomes the primary interface for Veekay Labs. Rather than browsing menus, visitors can interact conversationally to:

- Explore products
- Learn Product Management
- Learn AI Product Management
- Discover frameworks
- Navigate PMOS
- Review architecture diagrams
- Find GitHub repositories
- Build personalized learning journeys


### Knowledge Platform Evolution

The platform should gradually evolve from a collection of pages into a connected knowledge ecosystem.

Future capabilities include:

- Knowledge Graph connecting projects, articles, PMOS, demos, GitHub repositories, frameworks, and architecture.
- Automatic cross-linking between related content.
- Semantic search across all platform knowledge.
- AI-powered recommendations for related learning.
- Canonical content management with a single source of truth.

### Technical Evolution

As the platform grows, the architecture will evolve into:

- AI-native application architecture
- Multi-agent orchestration
- RAG-powered knowledge retrieval
- Hybrid keyword and semantic search
- Knowledge graph navigation
- API-first platform
- Modular services
- Architecture Decision Records (ADR)

### Content Evolution

Content will transition from individual pages into a governed knowledge ecosystem.

The long-term strategy includes:

- Single Source of Truth for every topic
- Build Once, Reuse Everywhere publishing model
- Evergreen knowledge base (PMOS)
- Builder's Journal documenting real product development
- Project case studies connected to articles and frameworks
- AI indexing of approved content only
- Continuous content improvement and versioning


### Platform Characteristics

Veekay Labs should ultimately become:

- A professional profile
- A living product portfolio
- A technical showcase
- A Product Management knowledge hub
- An AI Product Management learning platform
- An AI-native conversational experience
- A community-driven learning ecosystem

## Success Criteria

- End-to-end AI PM ecosystem available
- Community-driven content growth
- Open-source contributions established
- Platform recognized as a trusted AI Product Management resource
