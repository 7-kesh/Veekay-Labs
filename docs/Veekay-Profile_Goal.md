VK Labs Website Brief
VK Labs is an AI product strategy and experimentation studio led by an experienced AI Product Manager with cross-domain experience in healthcare, fintech, and automotive.
The core focus is helping organizations identify practical AI adoption opportunities inside complex business workflows without disrupting existing operations. VK Labs specializes in understanding how teams work today, finding bottlenecks and decision gaps, and shaping AI-enabled product solutions that improve efficiency, quality, and scalability.
The positioning should be credible, focused, and lightweight: this is not a large agency website. It is a professional founder profile and consulting page that explains the problem space, the expertise, and the kinds of engagements VK Labs can support.
