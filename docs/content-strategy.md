

# Veekay Labs
## Content Strategy

---

# Purpose

This document defines the content strategy for Veekay Labs. Every page, article, project, and AI interaction should reinforce the platform's mission: to showcase real product work, share practical knowledge, and help others learn Product Management and AI Product Management.

---

# Content Principles

## Authenticity Over Marketing
Publish real work, real lessons, real trade-offs, and measurable outcomes rather than promotional content.

## Build in Public
Document the journey from idea to production, including failures, pivots, evaluations, and improvements.

## Teach Through Experience
Use projects and case studies to explain concepts instead of relying only on theory.

## Evergreen First
Create foundational content that remains valuable over time, then complement it with timely updates.

## One Source of Truth
Each topic should have a canonical home. Articles, projects, demos, and AI responses should reference that source instead of duplicating content.

---

# Audience Strategy

## Recruiters
Need quick evidence of experience, impact, and leadership.

## Hiring Managers
Need deep case studies, business outcomes, and product thinking.

## Product Managers
Need frameworks, templates, and practical guidance.

## AI Engineers
Need architectures, evaluations, GitHub repositories, and implementation details.

## Learners
Need structured learning paths, PMOS, quizzes, and AI mentoring.

---

# Content Pillars

## Me
Professional profile, career journey, resume, leadership philosophy, certifications, and values.

## Build
Products, demos, PRDs, roadmaps, architecture diagrams, GitHub repositories, evaluation reports, and lessons learned.

## Think
Medium articles, Substack essays, LinkedIn posts, Builder's Journal, opinions, and product essays.

## Learn
PMOS, AI Product Management, Product Management, templates, playbooks, interview preparation, frameworks, and learning paths.

## Ask AI
Conversational access to all platform knowledge with grounded responses and recommendations.

---

# Homepage Content Strategy

The homepage should answer only four questions:

1. Who am I?
2. Why does my work matter?
3. What should visitors explore next?
4. How can they connect with me?

Avoid long biographies or excessive technical details.

---

# Project Content Template

Every project should include:

- Executive Summary
- Business Problem
- Users
- Product Vision
- Solution
- Product Decisions
- AI Decisions
- Architecture
- Demo
- Evaluation
- Business Impact
- Lessons Learned
- Roadmap
- Related Articles
- GitHub
- PRD

---

# Article Strategy

Organize articles into series instead of isolated posts.

Primary series:
- AI Product Management
- Product Management Fundamentals
- AI Engineering for PMs
- Evaluation & Responsible AI
- Builder's Journal
- Career & Leadership

Every article should link to related projects, PMOS topics, and follow-up reading.

---

# Knowledge Hub Strategy

PMOS serves as the canonical knowledge base.

Organize content into:
- Product Management
- AI Product Management
- LLMs
- Prompt Engineering
- RAG
- Agents
- Evaluation
- Metrics
- Leadership
- Templates
- Interview Prep

---

# AI Assistant Content Strategy

The AI Assistant should answer using indexed content from:
- Resume
- Projects
- Articles
- PMOS
- GitHub
- Builder's Journal
- Architecture documents
- Roadmaps

Responses should recommend related content and encourage deeper exploration.

---

# Content Lifecycle

Idea → Draft → Review → Publish → Cross-link → AI Index → Maintain → Archive

Every published asset should become part of the AI knowledge base.

---

# Publishing Cadence

- LinkedIn: Weekly insights
- Medium: Long-form essays
- Substack: Newsletter and reflections
- Builder's Journal: Weekly progress updates
- Projects: Updated as milestones are completed
- PMOS: Continuous improvement

---

# Success Metrics

Measure:
- Article readership
- Returning visitors
- AI Assistant engagement
- Project page engagement
- Knowledge Hub usage
- Newsletter growth
- Recruiter conversions
- Time spent learning

---

# Long-Term Vision

Content should evolve into a connected knowledge graph where projects, articles, PMOS, demos, GitHub repositories, and AI conversations reinforce one another. Visitors should be able to discover information through browsing, search, or natural conversation with the AI Assistant while always arriving at the authoritative source.
