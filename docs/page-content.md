## 10. Featured Projects

### ER Triage AI Co-pilot
- **Problem:** Emergency departments face overwhelming patient volumes and complex triage decisions, leading to inefficiencies and delays.
- **Solution:** Developed an AI-powered triage co-pilot that assists clinicians in prioritizing cases, leveraging real-time data and predictive analytics.
- **Impact:** Reduced triage time by 25%, improved patient outcomes, and optimized resource allocation.
- **Technologies:** NLP, predictive analytics, cloud-native architecture, FHIR interoperability.

### AI Document Parser
- **Problem:** Manual document processing in healthcare and finance is slow, error-prone, and resource-intensive.
- **Solution:** Built an AI document parser that extracts, validates, and structures data from unstructured documents using advanced machine learning models.
- **Impact:** Increased data extraction accuracy to 98%, reduced processing time by 60%, enabled seamless integration with downstream systems.
- **Technologies:** OCR, NLP, transformer models, Python, cloud APIs.

### Cloud Pharmacy Platform
- **Problem:** Traditional pharmacy systems are siloed and lack real-time data sharing, limiting patient care and operational efficiency.
- **Solution:** Led the development of a cloud-based pharmacy management platform supporting secure e-prescriptions, inventory, and analytics.
- **Impact:** Empowered 10,000+ pharmacists, improved medication safety, and delivered a 35% efficiency gain across operations.
- **Technologies:** Cloud-native services, FHIR standards, React, Node.js, secure APIs.

### Google Gemini Prescription Digitization
- **Problem:** Paper-based prescriptions slow down pharmacy workflows and increase the risk of errors.
- **Solution:** Integrated Google Gemini’s AI capabilities to digitize and validate prescriptions at scale, enabling automated processing and analytics.
- **Impact:** Reduced manual data entry, minimized errors, and accelerated prescription fulfillment for thousands of users.
- **Technologies:** Google Gemini, OCR, secure cloud integration, healthcare compliance.
