

# Veekay Labs

> **Build • Learn • Share • Elevate**

Veekay Labs is an AI-native platform that documents my journey of building AI products, sharing product thinking, and creating practical resources for Product Management and AI Product Management.

It begins as a professional profile and product portfolio, then evolves into a living builder platform, an AI Product Management knowledge hub, and ultimately an AI-powered learning ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Build an AI-native platform where visitors can:

- Discover my professional journey
- Explore real AI products
- Learn Product Management
- Learn AI Product Management
- Interact with an AI assistant
- Follow products from idea to production

---

# Core Pillars

## Me
Professional profile, experience, resume, career journey, leadership philosophy, and impact.

## Build
Real-world products, case studies, demos, architecture, evaluations, PRDs, GitHub repositories, and lessons learned.

## Think
Medium articles, Substack newsletters, LinkedIn posts, Builder's Journal, essays, and product frameworks.

## Learn
PMOS, templates, learning paths, interview preparation, AI Product Management resources, and practical guides.

## Ask AI
A conversational AI assistant that helps visitors explore my work, understand products, and learn Product Management.
